## Where to Start

<Callout type="info" title="New to this?">
Read [Patterns](/docs/patterns) first. It maps problem wording to technique, and once you can recognise the pattern, most problems stop being unfamiliar.

Then work through [Two Sum](/docs/arrays/two-sum), [Valid Parentheses](/docs/stacks-queues/valid-parentheses) and [Binary Search](/docs/searching/binary-search). Those three cover hash maps, stacks and the halving idea — the foundations for nearly everything else.
</Callout>
